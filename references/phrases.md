# Phrases to catch

Each phrase is a signal that a fact is missing. The fix is never a synonym. The fix is the missing fact.

## Empty praise
great job, good work, solid performance, strong contributor, rockstar, crushing it, exceeds expectations, a pleasure to work with, really stepped up, awesome

Fix: name what they did and what it made possible.

## Empty criticism
needs improvement, needs to step up, not a team player, lacks ownership, lacks initiative, not strategic enough, needs more executive presence, needs to level up, falls short

Fix: name the moment, the behavior, and what you expected instead.

## Personality labels
negative attitude, difficult, abrasive, passive, aggressive, arrogant, not a culture fit, too junior, too emotional

Fix: describe what was said or done, in which setting, and how others responded.

## Fuzzy frequency and size
sometimes, often, always, never, a lot, a bit, somewhat, quite, fairly, kind of, occasionally

Fix: give a count or a time window ("in 3 of the last 5 standups").

## Hollow business words
leverage, synergy, bandwidth, move the needle, think bigger, strategic thinking, ownership mindset, stakeholder management, drive impact, raise the bar

Fix: say the concrete thing, for example which decision, which stakeholder, which outcome.

## Hedges that dodge the message
I just wanted to mention, it might be worth considering, maybe you could possibly, no pressure but, take this with a grain of salt

Fix: state the request plainly once.

## Comparison without a basis
better than most, top performer, below peers, not at level

Fix: name the level expectation or the bar, and the gap against it.
